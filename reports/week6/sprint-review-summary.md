## Customer Feedback

### Questionnaire

* The questionnaire logic itself was not reviewed because planned question reordering had not yet been implemented.
* No additional questionnaire feedback was provided during this Sprint Review.

### Product Catalog

Positive feedback:

* Dynamic pricing works as expected.
* Database-driven product metadata is an improvement.

Requested improvements:

* Improve readability of gender labels.
* Consider replacing text labels with icons.

---

### Administrator Interface

The customer identified the admin interface as the main usability issue.

Feedback included:

* Too much manual text entry.
* Product creation is cumbersome.
* Dropdowns or predefined selections would be preferable.
* Reduce repetitive typing during product creation.

The customer emphasized that improving administrator usability would provide the greatest value.

---

### Product Configuration

The customer confirmed that the application should continue focusing on fragrance samples rather than full-size products.

Current sample sizes remain acceptable.

---

### Deployment

The customer advised the team not to spend additional effort on deployment or hosting before project delivery.

Priority should instead be given to improving the product itself.

---

## Requested Changes
