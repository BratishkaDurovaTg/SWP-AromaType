# Sprint Overview

## Sprint Goal

Complete follow-up maintenance after the Week 6 customer trial, address the remaining usability issues, finalize customer-facing documentation, complete the product transition, and deliver the final course version (MVP v3).

## Sprint Dates

**Start:** 2026-07-13

## Sprint Scope

# Sprint 5 Changes (MVP v3)

During Sprint 5 the team completed the final maintenance work required for MVP v3.

Implemented changes include:

- Removed unnecessary recommendation category filters.
- Fixed cart scrolling so products no longer overlap the checkout button.
- Added checkout validation with an appropriate error message when required fields are missing.
- Updated customer-facing documentation.
- Completed final handover documentation.
- Finalized repository documentation for course delivery.

# Final Transition Outcome

## Handover Level

**Ready for independent use.**

The customer confirmed that the final product, including the frontend, backend, and administration components, is ready for handover. The remaining transfer consists of providing repository access, deployment materials, and private administrator credentials.

## Customer Confirmation Status

**Accepted with follow-up items.**

The customer confirmed that the reported Sprint 5 issues had been resolved and requested completion of the remaining transfer activities, including repository access and delivery of private administration materials.

---

# Transition Summary

The project handover included the final application source code, frontend and backend components, administration system, repository contents, and customer documentation.

The customer confirmed that the final project package should include all materials produced during development. Repository access (or an archived project package), bot credentials, administrator credentials, and additional administrator guidance will be provided after the meeting through private communication where appropriate.

Customer-facing documentation is available in [docs/customer-handover.md](https://github.com/BratishkaDurovaTg/SWP-AromaType/blob/main/docs/customer-handover.md), which describes the delivered artifacts, deployment guidance, and responsibilities after handover.

At the conclusion of the project, no further development work remains under the team's responsibility as part of the course deliverables.

---

# Remaining Transition Items

No functional blockers preventing product handover were identified.

The customer requested completion of several administrative follow-up actions outside the public repository:

- transfer of repository access (or project archive);
- delivery of bot credentials and administrator credentials;
- delivery of additional administrator guidance describing perfume-to-psychotype mapping.

These items are administrative rather than development tasks and do not affect the delivered MVP functionality.

# Customer Usage

The customer confirmed readiness to receive the complete project package and continue working with the delivered system.
The team will provide the repository, application source code, administration materials, and private credentials required for customer-side operation. During the meeting, the customer confirmed that no additional functional changes were required before the final handover.
