## Purpose of Use

During Assignment 6 (Week 7), ChatGPT was used as a supporting tool for preparing the final project documentation. AI assistance was used to:

- improve wording, structure, and readability of documentation;
- assist with drafting documentation based on completed Sprint 5 work;
- review documentation against the Assignment 6 Week 7 requirements;
- improve English grammar and consistency;
- help organize and verify repository documentation before the final submission.

All software development, bug fixes, testing, Sprint activities, customer communication, product transition, deployment, repository management, and final delivery of MVP v3 were completed by the project team.

All documentation generated with AI assistance was reviewed, edited, and verified by the team before submission.
